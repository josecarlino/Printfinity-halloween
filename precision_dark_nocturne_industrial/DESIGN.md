## Brand & Style

This design system adapts an industrial, high-precision dark aesthetic into a sophisticated, minimalist Halloween atmosphere for a bespoke 3D printing and digital fabrication studio. The visual language moves decisively away from kitsch, plastic skeletons, or overt holiday tropes, channeling instead the moody, technical elegance of after-hours workshop machinery, thermal nozzle embers, and dark-room prototyping.

The aesthetic fuses **technical minimalism** with **dark industrial precision**. The interface remains razor-sharp, functional, and devoid of non-essential ornamentation, punctuated strategically by incandescent pumpkin orange accents and dark cinder violet subtleties. The emotional response is focused, enigmatic, and operationally exact: users experience the meticulous calm of an advanced additive manufacturing laboratory operating under late-night conditions.

## Colors

The color palette centers on absolute tonal discipline: a deep near-black void punctuated by surgical applications of thermal orange and occult cinders.

- **Base Canvas (`#0B0B0C`)**: An unyielding, low-reflectance foundation eliminating pure white glare while providing absolute contrast for technical telemetry.
- **Surfaces & Layers (`#161618`, `#1B1B1E`, `#201D24`)**: Elevated containers, side sheets, and cards occupy `#161618`. Deep cinder-violet undertones (`#201D24` and `#191024`) appear within active tooltips, segmented tracks, and contextual notifications to ground the Halloween theme without compromising technical utility.
- **Accents & Primary Vectors (`#FF7A00`, `#F97316`)**: Incandescent pumpkin orange drives interactive focal points, selected states, and real-time extruder/progress trackers.
- **Atmospheric Glow (`rgba(255, 122, 0, 0.15)`)**: Used exclusively on primary interactive elements during focus or active printing runtime, evoking the warm glow of filament extruders against nocturnal studio darkness.
- **Typography & Structural Lines**: High-legibility warm white (`#F5F5F5`) prevents optical fatigue against pitch backgrounds. Structural borders rest at `#26262A`, transitioning to `#3F3F46` on mouse interaction or `#FF7A00` for active selection.

## Typography

The typographic engine is built on **Inter**, prioritizing optical balance, structural neutral forms, and maximum numeric legibility across dark backgrounds.

- **Numerics & Technical Data**: All values concerning grams, print hours, slicing tolerances, and pricing must enable `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums` to ensure exact column alignment.
- **Hierarchy & Scale**: Large metrics use tighter tracking (`-0.025em`) for a monolithic, machined presence. Overline metadata and table headers utilize uppercase styling with generous tracking (`0.08em`) to enforce legibility at tiny scale.
- **Color Contrast**: Main body copy and headings use `#F5F5F5` for crisp readability. Secondary meta information sits at `#9A9AA2`, ensuring low-priority context does not compete with quantitative output.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is engineered around a disciplined **8px base grid** with a maximum container limit of `1200px` to maintain high scanability across dense 3D printing workflows.

- **Desktop (≥1024px)**: 12-column grid, 24px gutters, 32px lateral margins. Optimized for asymmetric tool arrangements (e.g., an 8-column slicing parameters input paired with a 4-column sticky production summary).
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px)**: 8-column layout, 16px gutters, 24px margins. Peripheral panels collapse below main operational queues.
- **Mobile (<768px)**: 4-column layout, 16px lateral padding. Tables gracefully transform into individual swipeable spec cards, and configuration drawers anchor as bottom sheets.

## Elevation & Depth

Spatial layering rejects traditional blurry drop shadows and chaotic skeuomorphism. Instead, physical presence is established through **calibrated surface luminance, 1px structural boundaries, and subtle localized luminescence**:

- **Layer 0 (Void Base)**: `#0B0B0C` — Default application canvas.
- **Layer 1 (Card & Module Foundation)**: `#161618` bordered by a 1px solid `#26262A` rule.
- **Layer 2 (Embedded & Interactive Substrates)**: `#1B1B1E` for nested configuration trays or hovered rows; deep violet ash `#201D24` for contextual callouts.
- **Layer 3 (Modals & Overlays)**: `#161618` surrounded by `1px solid #3F3F46`, elevated over a dimmed backdrop of `rgba(11, 11, 12, 0.85)` with a `12px` backdrop blur.
- **The Nocturne Filament Glow**: Primary interactive elements and active 3D printing monitors utilize a targeted, minimal amber glow: `box-shadow: 0 0 16px rgba(255, 122, 0, 0.15), 0 1px 2px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.60)`. This provides a radiant ambient indicator without introducing visual noise.

## Shapes

The geometric framework is calculated and ergonomic, avoiding both harsh brutalist points and overly playful rounded bubbles:

- **Component Standard (8px / `rounded-md`)**: Applied to all tactical inputs, buttons, segmented toggles, dropdowns, and form elements.
- **Container Standard (12px / `rounded-lg`)**: Applied to parent surface cards, data tables, metrics panels, and floating modals.
- **Pill (9999px / `rounded-full`)**: Strictly reserved for operational status badges (`Entregado`, `En proceso`, `Nuevo`) and filament swatch indicators.
